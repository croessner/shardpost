What happens when you visit

  • Server logs: our web server processes the data needed to deliver pages (IP address, time, requested URL, user agent, status) for operation and security, and deletes or anonymises them when no longer needed (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • No cookies, no analytics: all fonts, images and scripts come from shardpost.de. If you explicitly pick light or dark mode, your browser stores that choice under shardpost-theme in local storage; choosing “system” removes it.
  • Manual search: your browser loads a word index from shardpost.de once and searches it locally. Your search terms are not sent anywhere.
  • Contact: when you write to us, we use your message to answer it.
  • External links to GitHub, nauthilus.org or jmapper.de connect you to those providers only when you follow them. Public GitHub issues are visible to everyone.
