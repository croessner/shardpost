Before you ask

Many questions are answered in the manual. Good places to start:

  • Read-only first diagnosis in the operations guide.
  • The protocol reference, including what is not supported.
  • The manual pages for every shardpostctl command, with examples and exit codes.
  • The observability guide for what the metrics and logs mean.

A good bug report

  1. The ShardPost version (shardpostctl version) and the Ceph version.
  2. How it runs: Kubernetes with Rook or native, number of replicas, the authentication backend.
  3. What you did, what you expected and what happened instead, with the protocol commands if a client is involved.
  4. The relevant log lines. An IMAP protocol trace of one account (shardpostctl-trace(1)) often shows the problem directly.
Mind what you share. Operational logs contain client addresses and account names, and a protocol trace contains what the client sent. Remove passwords, tokens and private data before you post anything in a public issue.
