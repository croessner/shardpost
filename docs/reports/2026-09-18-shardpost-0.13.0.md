## Findings

- **Director v1.0.4 (source):**
  - A shard without a backend leads to `no backend for shard`, without a fallback
    to mailstack.
  - `failover.strategy` is not evaluated.
  - Held bindings without an active session are re-placed.
  - Operator pins are rejected on a shard change. Whether such pins
    existed was not checked via Redis; the acceptance afterwards ran without
    errors.
- **Nauthilus shadow:** before alpha.38, shadow ran a release older than the
  repo (versioned config ConfigMap). A repo apply would have triggered a downgrade.
  After alpha.38, both environments matched the repo, and only
  then were the manifests applied.
- **Reloader:** removing the old Nauthilus keys did not trigger a restart on
  prod. The owner then rolled Nauthilus out manually and
  verified it.
- **Nauthilus tests:** `make validate` in nauthilus-tests fails
  independently of this, on `check-mail-protocols` (`55-go-plugins.yml`).
