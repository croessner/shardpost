# ShardPost 0.35.1: ManageSieve with XOAUTH2 and OAUTHBEARER

Date: 2026-09-30

## Change

After the move of `de10000@srvint.net` its Sieve filters could not be
opened in Roundcube, which reported the Sieve server as unreachable.
Roundcube logs users in with OIDC, and the director authenticates to the
ManageSieve backend with the mechanism the client used; its log showed
`backend_auth_failed` with `mechanism=xoauth2` for `shardpost-sieve`,
and ShardPost saw no login attempt at all: its ManageSieve offered PLAIN
only. IMAP and POP3 already took XOAUTH2 and OAUTHBEARER.

ManageSieve now offers `PLAIN XOAUTH2 OAUTHBEARER`, with the token
checked like an IMAP token (`nauthilus.bearer_mode`). A failed token
gets `NO` at once, an authentication is bounded by 15 seconds, and a
client may cancel with `"*"`.

## Review

A review by a second model compared the change with RFC 5804 and
RFC 7628, the director's backend client and the ManageSieve library of
Roundcube. Its finding: the first version sent the error challenge of
RFC 7628 after a failed token, which neither the director nor Roundcube
reads — both would have waited for the `NO` until their timeout, so a
wrong token would have looked like a hang. The challenge was dropped.
It also noted that Nauthilus had never been asked for a token login
with the protocol `sieve`; the first real login after the rollout is
the check.
