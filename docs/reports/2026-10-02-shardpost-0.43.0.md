# ShardPost 0.43.0: delayed JMAP send (scaling stage R5), bench tool (R4)

Date: 2026-10-02

## Change

- **Delayed sending:** JMAP EmailSubmission accepts `HOLDFOR` and
  `HOLDUNTIL` (RFC 4865 FUTURERELEASE, as RFC 8621 §7 describes it);
  the session advertises `maxDelayedSend`, a pending submission can be
  canceled with `undoStatus: canceled`.
  - One admin-store record per submission and an entry in the due queue
    `submission`; states change only by compare-and-swap: `pending` →
    `sending` → `final` | `failed`, `pending` → `canceled`.
  - At most once: only the swap to `sending` starts a relay
    conversation. A conversation that broke during the transfer, or a
    record left in `sending` longer than `sending_timeout_seconds`,
    ends as final with an unknown result and is never retried.
  - The message is held as a copy of its own (intent `sub:` in the
    account registry), which purge, scrub and sweep know.
  - Workers hold partitions of the queue with a renewed lock and wake on
    a notify; nothing is sent for an account that is gone or being
    purged.
  - `shardpostctl submission list|cancel`, `GET /admin/v1/submissions`
    for records of all accounts (a diagnosis listing).
  - Configuration `jmap.submission.delayed.*`; off by default
    (`max_delay_seconds: 0`). Going back behind 0.43.0 needs an empty
    queue.
- **Bench tool:** `shardpost bench populate|measure` fills separate bench
  pools through the delivery pipeline and measures what an account
  costs in Ceph (objects, bytes, operations per delivery by object
  class, latency, compare-and-swap retries). It refuses the production
  pools, runs as a cephx user whose caps name the bench pools only, and
  is described in `docs/operations/bench.md`.

## Review

Delayed sending: three review rounds by a second model. Fixed among
others: an EmailSubmission that could stay pending for ever when the
worker was faster than the client's record, shutdowns that used up
retry attempts, partitions nobody held without a signal, a lock storm
on every notify, RFC 4865 parameter checks, live accounts without
directory entry taken for gone, a destroyed submission that could come
back, mail of an account being purged.

Bench: one review round (pool placement, PG count, load, guard), plus
the owner's decision to run it in separate pools with a cephx user of
its own.

## Rollout

1. 0.43.0 rolled out (server and push); no error or warning in the
   logs.
2. Delayed sending switched on in the overlay (`max_delay_seconds`
   604800, seven days).
3. `hack/r5-smoke.sh` (throwaway account): two submissions with
   `HOLDFOR=60`, one canceled; the other was scheduled on one server,
   sent by the other after its release and arrived; the canceled one
   did not.
4. imaptest: 72 groups, 0 failed, 8 skipped; base 0/460, extensions
   0/272. `hack/auth-smoke.sh` and `hack/r3-smoke.sh` passed again.
5. Bench pools and the bench cephx user created; the first bench run
   was refused because Rook's RBD initialisation leaves `rbd_info` and
   `rbd_trash` in a new pool. Both objects were removed from the empty
   bench pools by hand, and the guard now ignores them (commit after
   0.43.0, next release).
